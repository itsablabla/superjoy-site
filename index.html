Before you pick a plan

What counts as a task?

One request, however many steps it takes. Planning a trip across four tools is one task, not four.

Can I cancel later?

Any time, from Settings. You keep access through the end of the month you paid for.

Is my data used for training?

No. Your conversations stay yours — see the Privacy Policy for the detail.

What happens after early access?

Early access pricing is locked for your first year. We will tell you well before anything changes.
